AcmePay Privacy Incident Classification Policy

HIGH RISK

An incident must be classified as HIGH risk if the application log contains:
- Singapore NRIC or FIN identifiers
- Credit card numbers
- Other highly sensitive government or financial identifiers

If multiple PII types are present and at least one is HIGH risk, the entire incident is HIGH risk.

MEDIUM RISK

An incident must be classified as MEDIUM risk if the log contains:
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers

This classification applies when no HIGH-risk PII is present.

LOW RISK

An incident is LOW risk when no PII is detected.
